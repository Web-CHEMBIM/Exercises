# Classification of Questions from "4.3 Electric circuits MCQ QP.pdf"

This document maps all 98 questions from the question paper into the five subtopics for Module 17: Electric Circuits & Electrical Safety.

If a question spans multiple topics (e.g. combining series and parallel resistor calculations), it is placed in the most relevant advanced topic.
