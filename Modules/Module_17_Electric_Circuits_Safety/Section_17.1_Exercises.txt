  * Draw and interpret circuit diagrams containing standard components.

SECTION L: PREDICT AND EXPLAIN
47. Prediction: The lamp becomes brighter.
    Explanation: Heating the thermistor decreases its resistance, so the
    total resistance of the circuit decreases. This means more current
    flows, making the lamp brighter.
48. Prediction: The buzzer becomes quieter or stops sounding.
    Explanation: Without light, the LDR resistance increases greatly,
    reducing the current in the circuit. Less current means the buzzer
    receives insufficient energy to sound.
49. Prediction: The lamp goes out.
    Explanation: Reversing the cell reverses the direction of current.
    The diode is now in reverse bias and blocks the current, so no
    current flows and the lamp does not light.
50. Prediction: The other lamp goes out.
    Explanation: In a series circuit there is only one path for current.
    Removing one lamp breaks the circuit, so no current can flow and
    the remaining lamp goes out.
