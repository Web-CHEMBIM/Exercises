24. Rule: Current is the same at all points. This is because charge
    is conserved – electrons cannot be created or destroyed, so the
    same number passes through each component per second.
25. The total e.m.f. = 1.5 + 1.5 = 3.0 V. In series, e.m.f.s add
    together, not remain as one cell's value.
26. Total resistance increases, so current decreases. Since V = IR
    and I decreases, the p.d. across each original component decreases.
    The available voltage is now shared among more components.
27. The lamps have different resistances. The one with higher resistance
    has a larger p.d. across it (V = IR), so it dissipates more power
    and glows brighter.
