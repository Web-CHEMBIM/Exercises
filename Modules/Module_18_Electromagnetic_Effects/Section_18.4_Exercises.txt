================================================================================
                         MARK SCHEME
================================================================================

1. The motor effect. (Accept: force / electromagnetic force.)

2. (i) The conductor must be carrying a current.
   (ii) The conductor must be in an external magnetic field.
   (iii) The current must not be parallel to the field.
   (Force is greatest when current is perpendicular to field.)

3. (a) Force reverses direction — now into the page.
   (b) Force reverses direction — now into the page.
   (c) Force remains in the same direction (out of the page) — both reversals cancel.

4. Apparatus: short straight wire or conducting rail placed between two poles of
   a permanent magnet; connect to a d.c. power supply.
   Observation: when current is switched on, the wire jumps / moves in one direction.
   Reversing the current: wire jumps / moves in the opposite direction.
   Reversing the field (swap magnets): wire also moves in the opposite direction.
   (Award marks for sensible description of any suitable valid experiment.)

5. When the current is perpendicular (at 90°) to the magnetic field.

6. When the current is parallel to the magnetic field (no field lines are cut).

7. Thumb = direction of force (motion/thrust).
   First finger = direction of the magnetic field (from N to S).
   Second finger = direction of conventional current (+ to −).

8. (a) Using left-hand rule: First finger = right, Second finger = up → Thumb = out of page.
       Force is directed out of the page.
   (b) First finger = up, Second finger = into page → Thumb = to the right.
       Force is directed to the right.
   (c) First finger = out of page, Second finger = right → Thumb = downward.
       Force is directed downward.

9. (a) Electrons move to the right, so conventional current is to the LEFT.
   (b) First finger = out of page, Second finger = left → Thumb = downward.
       Force is directed downward.
   (c) The electrons curve downward (follow a curved / circular arc path).

10. The magnetic force always acts perpendicular to the direction of motion
    of the particle. A perpendicular force cannot do work on the particle,
    so it cannot change the particle's speed. It only changes the direction,
    causing the particle to curve (follow a circular arc).
