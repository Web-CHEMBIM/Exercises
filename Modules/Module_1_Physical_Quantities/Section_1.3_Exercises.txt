SECTION H: COMPARE & CONTRAST

44. Graphical method: Draw vectors to scale using a ruler and
    protractor, complete the rectangle, and measure the diagonal [B1].
    Calculation method: Use Pythagoras (R = √(F₁²+F₂²)) and
    trigonometry (θ = tan⁻¹(opp/adj)) [B1].
    Graphical method is visual and requires drawing equipment, but
    limited by drawing precision [B1]. Calculation method gives exact
    numerical answers and requires only a calculator, but needs
    mathematical skill [B1].

45. Equal perpendicular forces: resultant = F√2 and the angle is 45°
    to each force — the resultant bisects the right angle [B1+B1].
    Unequal perpendicular forces: resultant = √(F₁²+F₂²) and the
    angle is closer to the larger force [B1]. The larger force
    "pulls" the resultant direction towards itself [B1].
