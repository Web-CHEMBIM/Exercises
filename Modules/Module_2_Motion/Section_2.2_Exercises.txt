================================================================================
          SECTION 2.2 GRAPHS OF MOTION - PRACTICE EXERCISES
================================================================================

Learning Objectives Covered:
  * Sketch, plot and interpret distance-time and speed-time graphs.
  * Determine from data or graph shape when an object is at rest, moving
    with constant speed, accelerating or decelerating.
  * Calculate speed from a distance-time graph.
  * Calculate distance from the area under a speed-time graph.
  * Calculate acceleration from a speed-time graph.
  * Know what is meant by deceleration.

================================================================================
                    SECTION A: MULTIPLE CHOICE
================================================================================

1. On a distance-time graph, a horizontal line represents an object that is:
   A: Accelerating
   B: Moving at constant speed
   C: Stationary (at rest)
   D: Decelerating

2. On a distance-time graph, the gradient of the line gives:
   A: Acceleration
   B: Distance
   C: Speed
   D: Time

3. On a speed-time graph, the gradient of the line gives:
   A: Speed
   B: Distance
   C: Acceleration
   D: Time

4. The area under a speed-time graph represents:
   A: Acceleration
   B: Speed
   C: Force
   D: Distance travelled

5. A car decelerating is shown on a speed-time graph as:
   A: A horizontal line
   B: A line with positive gradient
   C: A line with negative gradient
   D: A curved line going upwards

6. On a distance-time graph, a straight line with a steep gradient
   indicates:
   A: The object is stationary
   B: The object is moving slowly
   C: The object is moving at a high constant speed
   D: The object is accelerating

7. Which of the following describes deceleration?
   A: Speeding up
   B: Constant speed
   C: Negative acceleration (slowing down)
   D: Changing direction

8. On a speed-time graph, a horizontal line above zero represents:
   A: An object at rest
   B: An object accelerating
   C: An object at constant speed
   D: An object decelerating

================================================================================
                    ANSWER KEY
================================================================================

1.  C (Stationary / at rest) - no change in distance
2.  C (Speed) - gradient = distance / time = speed
3.  C (Acceleration) - gradient = change in speed / time = acceleration
4.  D (Distance travelled) - area = speed × time = distance
5.  C (A line with negative gradient) - speed decreasing over time
6.  C (The object is moving at a high constant speed) - steeper = faster
7.  C (Negative acceleration / slowing down)
8.  C (An object at constant speed) - speed not changing

    (b) Speed = distance / time = 80 / 10 [1] = 8 m/s [1]
    (c) Speed = distance / time = (200 − 80) / (40 − 20) [1]

12. (a) a = Δv / Δt = (20 − 0) / (10 − 0) [1] = 20 / 10 = 2 m/s² [1]
    (b) The car is travelling at constant speed of 20 m/s [1].
    (c) Deceleration = Δv / Δt = (20 − 0) / (30 − 20) [1]
        = 20 / 10 = 2 m/s² [1]
    (d) Area under graph:
        Section 1 (triangle): ½ × 10 × 20 = 100 m [1]
        Section 2 (rectangle): 10 × 20 = 200 m [1]
        Section 3 (triangle): ½ × 10 × 20 = 100 m [1]
        Total = 100 + 200 + 100 = 400 m
