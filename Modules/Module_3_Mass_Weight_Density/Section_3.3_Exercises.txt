================================================================================
                    SECTION 3.3 EXERCISES
                    MEASURING DENSITY
================================================================================

  * Describe how to determine the density of a liquid.
  * Describe how to determine the density of a regularly shaped solid.
  * Describe how to determine the density of an irregularly shaped solid which
    sinks in a liquid (volume by displacement).

SECTION E: SHORT ANSWER (Sample Answers)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
21. ρ = m/V
    ρ = density (in g/cm³ or kg/m³)
    m = mass (in g or kg)
    V = volume (in cm³ or m³)

22. 1. Digital balance (to measure mass)
    2. Ruler (to measure dimensions)

23. Irregularly shaped objects do not have regular geometric shapes, so their
    volume cannot be calculated using mathematical formulas. The displacement
    method measures the volume of water displaced, which equals the volume
    of the object.

24. 1. Zero the balance before measuring / use the same balance throughout
    2. Read the measuring cylinder at eye level to avoid parallax error

25. Repeat readings help to identify and reduce random errors. Taking an
    average of multiple readings gives a more reliable result.
