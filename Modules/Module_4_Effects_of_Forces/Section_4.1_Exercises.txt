================================================================================
          SECTION 4.1 RESULTANT FORCES - PRACTICE EXERCISES (50 Questions)
================================================================================

Learning Objectives Covered:
  * State the effects of forces on an object (changes in speed, direction, shape).
  * Identify different types of forces and describe their effects.
  * Know that forces can be balanced or unbalanced.
  * Calculate the resultant force of two or more forces acting along the same line.

Total Questions: 50
Assessment Objectives: AO1 (25%), AO2 (50%), AO3 (25%)

================================================================================

1. Which of the following is NOT a possible effect of a force?
   A: Changing an object's mass
   B: Changing an object's speed
   C: Changing an object's shape
   D: Changing an object's direction

2. Two forces act on an object: 5 N to the right and 3 N to the left.
   What is the resultant force?
   A: 2 N to the left
   B: 2 N to the right
   C: 8 N to the left
   D: 8 N to the right

3. A book rests on a table. Which statement describes the forces acting on it?
   A: Only gravity acts on it
   B: Only the table pushes up on it
   C: The forces are unbalanced
   D: The forces are balanced

4. Force is a vector quantity. This means it has:
   A: Magnitude only
   B: Direction only
   C: Magnitude and direction
   D: Mass and direction

5. Which unit is used to measure force?
   A: Kilogram (kg)
   B: Joule (J)
   C: Newton (N)
   D: Watt (W)

6. Three forces act on a block along the same line: 
   10 N right, 4 N left, and 6 N left.
   What is the resultant force?
   A: 0 N
   B: 2 N right
   C: 20 N left
   D: 10 N left

7. An object has balanced forces acting on it. Which statement is correct?
   A: The object must be stationary
   B: The object must be accelerating
   C: The resultant force is zero
   D: The object must be moving

8. A force of 12 N acts to the right and a force of 5 N acts to the left.
   What is the magnitude of the resultant force?
   A: 7 N
   B: 17 N
   C: 60 N
   D: 2.4 N

9. Which of the following situations involves balanced forces?
   A: A car accelerating from rest
   B: A ball falling through the air
   C: A parachutist falling at constant velocity
   D: A rocket launching upwards

10. Four forces act on an object: 8 N north, 3 N south, 2 N south, and 5 N north.
    What is the resultant force?
    A: 8 N north
    B: 8 N south
    C: 18 N north
    D: 2 N south
