   forward. What is the total backward force (friction + air resistance)?
   A: 0 N
   B: 5000 N
   C: 10000 N
   D: Cannot be determined

7. Which quantity must be constant for Newton's first law to apply?
   A: Speed only
   B: Direction only
   C: Velocity (speed and direction)
   D: Acceleration

8. An ice hockey puck slides across frictionless ice at constant speed. 
   According to Newton's first law, what will happen?
   A: It will slow down gradually
   B: It will speed up
   C: It will continue at constant speed indefinitely
   D: It will stop immediately

9. A parachutist falls at constant velocity (terminal velocity). What does this 
   tell us about the forces?
   A: Air resistance is greater than weight
   B: Weight is greater than air resistance
   C: Air resistance equals weight
   D: No forces are acting

10. Which of the following requires a resultant force?
    A: An object at rest
    B: An object moving at constant velocity
    C: An object changing direction
    D: An object in equilibrium
