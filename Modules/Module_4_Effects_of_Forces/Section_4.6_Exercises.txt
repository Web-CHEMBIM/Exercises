Learning Objectives Covered:
  * Understand that a force is required to produce circular motion.
  * Describe how mass, speed and radius affect the required force.
  * Explain why velocity changes in circular motion even at constant speed.

Total Questions: 50
Assessment Objectives: AO1 (25%), AO2 (50%), AO3 (25%)

                    SECTION A: MULTIPLE CHOICE QUESTIONS (10)
================================================================================

1. An object travels in a circle at constant speed. Which statement is correct?
   A: Its velocity is constant
   B: Its velocity is changing
   C: No forces act on it
   D: It is not accelerating

2. The force that keeps an object moving in a circle is called:
   A: Centrifugal force
   B: Gravitational force (only)
   C: Centripetal force
   D: Frictional force (only)

3. In which direction does the centripetal force act?
   A: Away from the center of the circle
   B: Towards the center of the circle
   C: In the direction of motion
   D: Opposite to the direction of motion

4. Ideally, the angle between the velocity vector and the centripetal force is:
   A: 0°
   B: 45°
   C: 90°
   D: 180°

5. If the speed of an object in circular motion increases (mass and radius constant), 
   the required force:
   A: Increases
   B: Decreases
   C: Stays the same
   D: becomes zero

6. If the radius of the circle increases (mass and speed constant), the required 
   force:
   A: Increases
   B: Decreases
   C: Stays the same
   D: Doubles

7. A satellite orbits the Earth. What provides the centripetal force?
   A: Friction
   B: Tension
   C: Gravity
   D: Air resistance

8. A car turns a corner on a flat road. What provides the centripetal force?
   A: Gravity
   B: Friction between tires and road
   C: Air resistance
   D: The engine

9. An object moves in a circle. If the centripetal force suddenly disappears, 
   the object will:
   A: Stop immediately
   B: Spiral into the center
   C: Fly off in a straight line along the tangent
   D: Continue in the circle

10. Which change requires the greatest increase in centripetal force?
    A: Doubling the mass
    B: Doubling the radius
    C: Doubling the speed
    D: Halving the mass
