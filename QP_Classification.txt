# Classification of Questions from "4.3 Electric circuits QP.pdf"

This document maps all 33 structured, multi-part questions from the question paper into the five subtopics for Module 17: Electric Circuits & Electrical Safety.

If a question spans multiple topics (e.g. combining series and parallel resistor calculations), it is placed in the most relevant advanced topic.

## 17.1 Circuit Diagrams & Components
*Questions testing knowledge of drawing/interpreting circuit symbols, ammeter/voltmeter positioning, relays, thermistors, LDRs, and component functions.*
Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Q5, Q6, Q9, Q10, Q11, Q12, Q17

## 17.2 Series Circuits
*Questions testing the behavior of current, e.m.f. of cells in series, potential difference, combined resistance in series, and drawing current vs resistance graphs.*
Q7, Q8, Q13, Q22

## 17.3 Parallel Circuits
*Questions addressing multiple branches, the junction rule, current splitting, calculating combined resistance in parallel networks, and advantages of parallel lighting.*
Q14, Q16, Q18, Q19, Q20, Q24, Q25, Q27, Q30

## 17.4 Potential Dividers
*Questions focusing on variable potential dividers, the splitting of voltage based on resistance ratio ($R_1 / R_2 = V_1 / V_2$), and input sensors (LDR/thermistor) producing variable output voltages in complex circuits.*
Q15, Q21, Q23, Q26, Q28, Q29, Q31, Q33

## 17.5 Electrical Safety
*Questions concerning hazards, mains wiring (live, neutral, earth), correct placement of switches and fuses, and calculating suitable fuse ratings based on power/current.*
Q32
